👋 About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the School of Automation, Southeast University. My research focuses on semantic correspondence based on vision foundation models and neuroscience-informed decoding for functional near-infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS)-based brain–computer interfaces.

Research interests: computer vision; semantic correspondence; brain–computer interfaces; fNIRS.
